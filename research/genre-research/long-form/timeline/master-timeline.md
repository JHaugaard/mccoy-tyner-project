# Master timeline: 1945–1985

Two layers, kept separate on purpose.


**Layer 1 (hard data).** Album density per year in the live canon, derived from
`jazz-canon/app/public/data/albums.json`, 254 albums. This is what John actually
holds, so it is the most honest guide to how *he* sees the history's weight.


**Layer 2 (interpretive).** Anchor events. Each is tagged with a confidence mark:
`[verified]` = I retrieved a source this session; `[common]` = widely agreed but not
sourced by me here; `[unverified]` = treat as a lead to check before publishing.

**Eight densest years:** 1960 (23), 1964 (20), 1956 (18), 1961 (18), 1963 (18), 1957 (16), 1962 (16), 1958 (15)


The canon peaks in the late fifties and sixties and thins sharply after 1972.
That is partly a judgement about the music and partly a fact about a Blue Note-
dominated collection — worth remembering when the page implies the history ends.


### First appearance of each style in the canon

| Style | First canon year | Albums in that year |
|---|---|---|
| Bebop | 1945 — *The Charlie Parker Story* | 2 |
| Cool Jazz | 1950 — *Subconscious-Lee* | 2 |
| Hard Bop | 1952 — *The Amazing Bud Powell, Vol. 1* | 3 |
| Post-Bop | 1956 — *Pithecanthropus Erectus* | 18 |
| Modal Jazz | 1958 — *At the Pershing: But Not for Me* | 15 |
| Soul Jazz | 1958 — *The Sermon!* | 15 |
| Free Jazz | 1960 — *Change of the Century* | 23 |
| Jazz-Rock | 1969 — *Emergency!* | 8 |
| Fusion | 1970 — *Red Clay* | 7 |
| Avant-Garde Jazz | 1972 — *Conference of the Birds* | 5 |
| Jazz-Funk | 1973 — *Head Hunters* | 2 |
| European Jazz | 1973 — *Witchi-Tai-To* | 2 |

Read as a sequence, this is the canon's own arrival order, and it is *not* the
history's order: cool jazz (1950) arrives before hard bop (1952) and modal (1958),
and avant-garde jazz (1972) arrives almost last, long after free jazz (1960) and
after the electric styles (jazz-rock, 1969). Filing order and
musical order are different things — a good argument for the page's 'these are
conversations, not a queue' framing.

### The five dates that carry the most argumentative weight

If the page needs only a handful of dates, these are the ones that do real work:

- **1945** — the canon begins, in bebop.
- **1950** — the canon's *first move* is the reaction against bebop. The collection's centre
  of gravity is already a response to something.
- **1959** — *Kind of Blue* and *The Shape of Jazz to Come* in the same year. Modal and
  free-jazz-adjacent arrive together and diverge immediately. Nothing else in the timeline
  shows the split this sharply.
- **1964/65** — *A Love Supreme* to *Ascension*, six months apart, same musician.
- **1970** — *Bitches Brew*: electric without abandoning the changes. The event that most
  often gets filed under the wrong axis.
