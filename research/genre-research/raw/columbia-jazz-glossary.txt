### A

**[a cappella](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/a_cappella.html)** \- Sung without instrumental accompaniment....

**[AABA](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/aaba.html)** \- The most common popular song form. \*...

**[accent](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/accent.html)** \- A note or tone that is given stress by volume or attack....

**[acid jazz](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/acid_jazz.html)** \- Music for dancing, first heard in the 1980s, that combines elements of soul jazz, funk, and hip hop, and mixes acoustic and electric instruments. (See also groove and club jazz.)...

**[ad lib](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/ad_lib.html)** \- Also "ad libitum." A notation on written music that gives the performer freedom to vary the notes or tempo; in jazz it typically means to improvise freely....

**[air check](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/air_check.html)** \- A musical radio broadcast that was originally recorded for distribution to other stations; radio broadcasts that people have recorded off the radio that are sometimes released commercially or bootlegged....

**[all-in](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/all_in.html)** \- The last chorus (in older jazz), often louder and more vigorous than the rest, and played by the ensemble....

**[altered chord](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/altered_chord.html)** \- A dominant chord that has the 5th or 9th raised or lowered by a single semi-tone....

**[alternate takes](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/alternate_takes.html)** \- The various takes recorded of a piece of music at a single recording session, that for whatever reasons were not chosen to be used. (A version recorded on a different day is not an alternate take.)...

**[arco](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/arco.html)** \- Playing a string instrument with the bow, instead of pizzicato....

**[arpeggio](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/arpeggio.html)** \- Sounding the individual notes of a chord quickly, one at a time, usually starting at the lowest note....

**[arrangement](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/arrangement.html)** \- An adaptation of a musical composition. Arrangements may be as minimal as a bass line or as complex as a full orchestral score. An arranger may take such great liberties with the original piece that it becomes a new composition....

**[arranger](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/arranger.html)** \- A person who writes arrangements....

**[arrhythmic](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/arrhythmic.html)** \- Without an obvious beat....

**[articulation](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/articulation.html)** \- The style in which a tone is produced, i.e., with slurs, staccato, variations in volume, and the like....

**[atonal](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/atonal.html)** \- Without a tonal center....

**[attack](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/attack.html)** \- The manner in which a tone is articulated....

**[avant-garde jazz](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/avant_garde_jazz.html)** \- A term loosely applied to various forms of "experimental" jazz first heard in the 1950s, and their later offshoots, especially in the sixties and seventies (see free jazz)...

**[ax](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/a/ax.html)** \- Also "axe." Any musical instrument....

### B

**[ballad](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/ballad.html)** \- A slow song, usually of a romantic nature; sometimes used for any song of the AABA or similar popular song form....

**[bar](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bar.html)** \- Also known as measure. A grouping of beats, that establishes the meter of a piece of music....

**[barrelhouse](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/barrelhouse.html)** \- An older style of piano, rough, loud, and appropriate to playing in noisy bars and dance halls....

**[bass drum](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bass_drum.html)** \- Also "kick drum." The largest and lowest-pitched drum of the drum set, and played with a foot pedal....

**[beat](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/beat.html)** \- A heard or felt pulse of a piece of music....

**[bebop](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bebop.html)** \- Also "bop." A style of jazz characterized by long flowing melodic lines, irregular accents, non-symmetrical written themes, and elaborated harmonies; first heard c. 1943....

**[behind the beat](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/behind_the_beat.html)** \- Playing slightly behind the beat as articulated by the rhythm section or implied by the ensemble....

**[big band](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/big_band.html)** \- An orchestra of more than 10 members....

**[bitonality](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bitonality.html)** \- The use of two different keys at once....

**[block chords](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/block_chords.html)** \- A series of chords with wide voicings that move in parallel motion. (See also locked hands.)...

**[blow](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/blow.html)** \- To improvise (on any instrument); to play....

**[blue notes](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/blue_notes.html)** \- (1) Pitches in the scale that can be flattened or sharpened within the blues scale; (2) tones that are bent or changed to increase the expressivity of the music, not simply to alter the scale....

**[blues](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/blues.html)** \- (1) A 12-bar form built on the I, IV, and V chords; (2 ) a scale with a flatted third, fifth, and perhaps a seventh; (3) a poetic form; (4) a way of articulating tones; (5) a set of verbal...

**[bolero](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bolero.html)** \- Originally, a Cuban mid-tempo form played by guitar trios; now more generally a slower and more sentimental form (Latin)....

**[bombs](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bombs.html)** \- Irregular bass drum accents (typical of bebop drummers)....

**[boogaloo](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/boogaloo.html)** \- Also bugalú. A rhythm and blues and soul-influenced Latin form originated in the United States and characterized by elements of mambo and chachachá with an added back beat....

**[boogie woogie](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/boogie_woogie.html)** \- A style of piano blues based on strong left hand eighth note figures. First heard in the late 1920s, but popular throughout the 1940s....

**[book](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/book.html)** \- The repertoire of a band or singer....

**[boot it](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/boot_it.html)** \- To play with energy and excitement (early jazz)....

**[bootleg](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bootleg.html)** \- Recordings or made or sold without the permission of the performers or a recording company....

**[bop](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bop.html)** \- See bebop....

**[bossa nova](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bossa_nova.html)** \- A Brazilian jazz/pop music form derived from the samba (originated c. 1960), influenced by cool jazz, and usually played quietly, with minimal percussion....

**[bounce](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bounce.html)** \- A light, medium fast tempo piece (swing era)....

**[box](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/box.html)** \- A piano; a guitar....

**[break](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/break.html)** \- A short suspension of rhythm or the flow of the music (usually of a four or eight beat duration) while the soloist or melody instruments continue playing....

**[bridge](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/bridge.html)** \- The third group of eight-bars in a thirty-two bar chorus (see popular song form); also known as the channel, the middle-eight or the B-section....

**[brushes](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/b/brushes.html)** \- Drum sticks with wire brushes on the end, sued to produce a quieter, scratching sound....

### C

**[call-and-response](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/call_and_response.html)** \- An antiphonal pattern common to jazz and all African American folk music, with a "call" played by a soloist and "answered" by the ensemble....

**[chachacha](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chachacha.html)** \- Also "chachachá." A mambo and/or danzón-derived rhythmic style and dance form (Latin)....

**[changes](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/changes.html)** \- A series of chords; the harmonic structure of a piece of music; the chords for a particular melody....

**[charanga](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/charanga.html)** \- Originally a Cuban orchestra of a flute, violins, and rhythm section, now more often with brass instruments added....

**[chart](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chart.html)** \- A musical arrangement....

**[chase](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chase.html)** \- A series of short musical passages (trading fours or twos) played by several players at a fast tempo....

**[Chicago style jazz](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chicago_style_jazz.html)** \- A style of small band jazz popular in Chicago in the 1920s and 1930s that derived from New Orleans style, but emphasized greater solo space, fixed ensembles, and a more prominent role for the rhythm section....

**[chops](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chops.html)** \- Technical ability; the lips of a brass player....

**[chord](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chord.html)** \- The simultaneous sounding of three or more tones....

**[chord progression](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chord_progression.html)** \- See changes....

**[chorus](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chorus.html)** \- The refrain or the main body of a popular song. See popular song forms....

**[chromaticism](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/chromaticism.html)** \- The use of all 12 tones of a scale...

**[circle of fifths](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/circle_of_fifths.html)** \- A series of twelve perfect fifths that circle back to the original tone....

**[circular breathing](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/circular_breathing.html)** \- A technique used by wind instrument players and singers to produce a continuous stream of notes without stopping for air. (The air is inhaled through the nose simultaneously while the mouth continues to produce musical sounds.)...

**[clave](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/clave.html)** \- A five-beat pattern that underlies all salsa music....

**[claves](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/claves.html)** \- A pair of wooden sticks used to play the clave pattern (Latin)....

**[coda](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/coda.html)** \- The conclusion to a piece of music that functions like a summing-up, or an afterthought. A short coda is called a tag....

**[collective improvisation](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/collective_improvisation.html)** \- Simultaneous improvisation by several musicians (most often heard in early jazz and free jazz)....

**[combo](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/combo.html)** \- A small instrumental group of fewer than ten musicians....

**[comping](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/comping.html)** \- The pattern of rhythmic placement of harmony used by keyboardists and guitarists while accompanying soloists....

**[conjunto](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/conjunto.html)** \- "Combo": a band of guitar, tres, bass, bongos, trumpets, piano, percussion, and three vocalists, first formed for playing in Cuban carnival (Latin)....

**[contrapuntal](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/contrapuntal.html)** \- See counterpoint....

**[cool jazz](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/cool_jazz.html)** \- A jazz style characterized by moderate volume, quiet rhythm sections, low vibrato, and sometimes improvised counterpoint; c. 1950s. (See also West Coast jazz.)...

**[coro](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/coro.html)** \- "Chorus": the two or three-voice refrain sung against a montuno....

**[coro/pregón](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/coro_pregon.html)** \- Call-and-response between soloist and the coro (Latin). \\...

**[counterpoint](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/counterpoint.html)** \- Independent improvised or composed melodies played against each other....

**[cross rhythm](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/cross_rhythm.html)** \- The simultaneous use of two or more different rhythmic patterns; a basic feature of most African American musics....

**[cut/cutting/carving](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/c/cut.html)** \- To outplay other musicians, usually in a jam session....

### D

**[danzon](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/danzon.html)** \- Also danzón. A 19th century Cuban dance derived from European contredances; a musical form usually played by a charanga (Latin)....

**[descarga](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/descarga.html)** \- A Latin jam session....

**[dirty tone](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/dirty_tone.html)** \- A description used in the in the 1920s and 1930s for horn players with a rough, noisy tone quality....

**[Dixieland](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/dixieland.html)** \- Also "Dixie." A term popularly applied to players (especially from the North) who continued in the New Orleans' jazz tradition....

**[double](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/double.html)** \- The ability to play more than one instrument....

**[double-time](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/doubletime.html)** \- A doubling of tempo in the melody while the accompanying instruments remain at the slower tempo; or all the instruments doubling the tempo together. This is a common rhythm device in ballad playing....

**[down-home](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/downhome.html)** \- Music that is honest, folk-like, and possibly funky....

**[downbeat](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/downbeat.html)** \- The first beat of a measure....

**[drone](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/drone.html)** \- Another name for pedal point....

**[drum set](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/drum_set.html)** \- Also "drum kit." A collection of drums, cymbals, and various other percussion instruments played by a single drummer; includes a bass drum, tom-toms, snare drum, hi-hat cymbal, ride cymbal, etc....

**[dub](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/dub.html)** \- A copy of another recording....

**[dynamics](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/d/dynamics.html)** \- Different degrees of volume and intensity....

### F

**[fake](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/fake.html)** \- To play without written music....

**[fake book](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/fake_book.html)** \- A book of music that contains only the melodies and chord progressions of popular songs and well-known jazz compositions....

**[false fingering](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/false_fingering.html)** \- A technique of altered finger placement that produces tones or density of sound on horns that are not available by orthodox techniques....

**[fills](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/fills.html)** \- Short improvised passages behind a soloist or between sections of a piece of music....

**[flatted fifth](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/flatted_fifth.html)** \- The lowering (by a half-step) of the fifth degree of a chord; a device especially associated with early bebop....

**[formulaic improvisation](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/formulaic_improvisation.html)** \- The use of a wide variety of elements (including favorite licks or fragments, manipulations of intervals and range, interpolated phrases, etc.) in developing a solo....

**[four-beat](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/fourbeat.html)** \- Also playing in four. A form of rhythm organization in which all four beats are relatively equal. Four-beat was especially common in the swing era and afterwards, but was also found in earlier jazz....

**[free jazz](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/free_jazz.html)** \- A cluster of jazz styles (post-1954) that minimize the importance of a fixed beat and a given harmonic structure, and emphasize the sound and texture of music....

**[front line](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/front_line.html)** \- The horns; all the instruments but the rhythm section....

**[funk](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/f/funk.html)** \- Also "funky." A loose term for music that draws from blues- or gospel-based harmony, rhythm, and melody; also (since the 1960s) a complex, bass and rhythm guitar-driven, sometimes three-against-four pattern, with horns used in rhythm patterns and shouted vocals....

### H

**[half-time](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/halftime.html)** \- A rhythmic device in which the melody continues at a fixed tempo while the accompanying instruments double the tempo....

**[hard bop](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/hard_bop.html)** \- A loose term for a large variety of jazz styles that appeared after bebop in the 1950s....

**[harmony](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/harmony.html)** \- Simultaneous sounding of two or more tones....

**[head arrangement](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/head_arrangement.html)** \- A musical arrangement made up (usually collectively) during a performance....

**[hi-hat cymbal](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/hihat_cymbal.html)** \- Two cymbals on a single rod that snap together when operated by a foot pedal....

**[hocket](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/hocket.html)** \- The division of a melody into separate parts for different voices or instruments, resulting in a kind of cross-talk....

**[homophony](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/homophony.html)** \- An individualized and loose form of unison, especially in early jazz....

**[honk](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/honk.html)** \- A low note played loudly on a reed instrument. (See also overblowing)....

**[hot](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/h/hot.html)** \- Hot jazz (as distinct from the music of sweet bands or commercial music) was a name for early jazz....

### I

**[improvisation](http://ccnmtl.columbia.edu/projects/jazzglossary/i/improvisation.html)** \- Music created in the moment of performance, without written scores or played from memory....
