_**Free Jazz: A Collective Improvisation**_ is a [studio album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_album "Studio album") by the American [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz") saxophonist [Ornette Coleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ornette_Coleman "Ornette Coleman"), released in September 1961 by [Atlantic Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Records "Atlantic Records"). The [recording session](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recording_session "Recording session") took place on December 21, 1960, at A&R Studios in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City "New York City"). The sole outtake from the album session, "First Take," was later released on the 1971 compilation _[Twins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twins_(Ornette_Coleman_album) "Twins (Ornette Coleman album)")_ and subsequent CD reissues of _Free Jazz_.
